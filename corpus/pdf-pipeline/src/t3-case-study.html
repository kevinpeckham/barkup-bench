Quartzline Yard OS
Customer Case Study

Pallet & Porter Cuts Trailer Detention 58% Across 23 Distribution Yards

Industry: Regional grocery distribution  ·  Fleet: 410 tractors, 1,900 trailers  ·  Deployment: 23 yards in 11 months  ·  Go-live: March 2025

The Challenge

Pallet & Porter's yards ran on whiteboards and radio calls. Gate clerks logged arrivals on paper; spotters learned of moves by driving the lot. During the 2024 holiday peak, average trailer detention reached 4.6 hours, and the carrier surcharges that followed erased most of the quarter's transportation savings.

Worse, nobody could answer the simple question "where is trailer 8841?" without a physical yard walk. Perishable loads were twice discovered after their reefer fuel ran out — a six-figure loss each time and a compliance report to a major retail partner.

The Solution

Quartzline deployed its Yard OS with solar RTLS tags on every trailer and a gate kiosk that cut check-in to under a minute. Spotter moves are now dispatched by the optimizer, which sequences work against dock schedules and reefer fuel telemetry rather than radio seniority.

Rollout ran region by region — three pilot yards in six weeks, then a template-driven expansion handled by two Quartzline engineers and Pallet & Porter's own ops trainers. No yard closed for cutover; the longest gate downtime recorded was eleven minutes.

“We used to staff an extra spotter per shift just to find trailers. Now the system knows where everything is before the driver has parked — my gate team found two hours a day they didn't know they had.”
Reina Calloway · VP of Distribution Operations, Pallet & Porter Foods
