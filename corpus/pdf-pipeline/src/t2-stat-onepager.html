2025: The year our middle-mile network stopped being the bottleneck

Over twelve months, Harborline consolidated 14 regional cross-dock facilities into 9 automated hubs, re-routed 61% of linehaul volume onto dynamic schedules, and moved dock-assignment decisions from nightly batch planning to a rolling 15-minute horizon. The result was the strongest service year in company history — achieved while absorbing an 18% increase in parcel volume and the addition of two national retail accounts. The figures below cover January through December 2025 across all 41 service regions.
