ReRollout of the Unified Claims Intake Platform

Unified Claims Intake Platform — Phase Two Begins April 6

Following a successful six-month pilot in the Mountain West region, the Unified Claims Intake Platform will begin its phase-two rollout on Monday, April 6. Offices in the Pacific, Southwest, and Great Lakes regions will transition first, with remaining regions following in three-week intervals through the end of July. Pilot offices processed first-notice-of-loss reports 31 percent faster on average, and customer callbacks for missing documentation fell by nearly half.

Each office will receive two days of on-site training the week before its cutover date. Training cohorts are capped at twelve adjusters, so managers should expect staggered scheduling across the full week. Legacy intake queues will remain readable for ninety days after cutover, but no new claims may be opened in the old system once an office goes live. Supervisors should complete the readiness checklist distributed by Regional IT no later than ten business days before their scheduled date.

Questions about scheduling should go to your regional operations lead; technical questions belong with the platform team at extension 4410. We know a transition of this size asks a lot of busy claims teams, and the operations group has committed to daily office hours during each region's first two weeks. Thank you for the care you have shown policyholders throughout the pilot — it is the reason this program is moving forward ahead of schedule.

Dana Whitfield
Vice President, Claims Operations · Hartwell City Headquarters
