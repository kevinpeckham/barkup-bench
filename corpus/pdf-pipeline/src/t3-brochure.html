Frequently Asked Questions

How long does switching from our current provider take?
Most teams finish in five to ten business days. We import your year-to-date figures, run one shadow payroll beside your old provider, and you approve cutover only when the two match to the cent.
What happens if a filing is wrong?
If Beacon Pay makes a filing error, we pay the penalty and interest, file the amendment, and tell you before the agency does. That guarantee is in the contract on every plan, including Starter.
Can we pay international contractors?
Yes — contractors in 41 countries can be paid in local currency on the Growth and Scale plans. Beacon Pay handles the exchange and produces a single reconciled ledger entry per run.
Is there a long-term contract?
No. All plans are month to month, and you can export every record — payslips, filings, and journals — at any time in standard formats.
